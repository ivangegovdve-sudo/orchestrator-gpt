# Weekly AI/ML Glossary Terms (2026-10-12)

Added three AI/ML terms to /tmp/glossary-current.md:

## Fine-Tuning

Definition: Fine-tuning is the process of adapting a pretrained model by training it further on a focused dataset for a particular task, behavior, or domain.

Explanation: It can improve performance on specialized objectives while requiring much less data and compute than training a model from scratch. Poorly chosen data or excessive training can reduce general capabilities or cause the model to overfit.

## Embeddings

Definition: Embeddings are numerical vector representations that encode meaningful relationships among text, images, audio, or other data.

Explanation: Items with similar properties are typically positioned near one another in the resulting vector space. Embeddings support semantic search, recommendations, clustering, classification, and retrieval-augmented generation.

## Diffusion Models

Definition: Diffusion models are generative models that learn to create data by reversing a gradual process that adds noise to training examples.

Explanation: During generation, the model starts with random noise and repeatedly denoises it into a sample that resembles the learned data distribution. They are widely used for image, audio, video, and other forms of synthetic content generation.
