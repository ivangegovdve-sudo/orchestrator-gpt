# Weekly AI/ML Glossary Terms (2026-08-03)

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
A technique that enhances large language models by retrieving relevant information from external knowledge bases before generating responses. This approach combines the parametric knowledge of LLMs with non-parametric external data sources to improve factual accuracy, reduce hallucinations, and enable up-to-date responses without requiring frequent model retraining.

## Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT)
A set of techniques that adapt large pre-trained models to downstream tasks by updating only a small subset of parameters while keeping the majority frozen. PEFT methods like LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) and adapters significantly reduce computational costs and storage requirements compared to full fine-tuning, making it feasible to customize massive models on limited hardware.

## Mixture of Experts (MoE)
An architecture that routes input tokens to specialized sub-networks (experts) based on their content, activating only a fraction of the model's total parameters per token. This approach enables massive model scaling while maintaining computational efficiency, as seen in models like Switch Transformers and Mixtral, where different experts specialize in different types of linguistic patterns or knowledge domains.
