# Weekly AI/ML Glossary Terms (2026-07-20)

## Added Terms

### **Active Learning Bias**
#### Definition
Predictive model accuracy is consistently improved through targeted human feedback.
#### Explanation
This concept arises from the idea that active learning strategies often rely on expert annotators to provide feedback, which can introduce a bias towards more difficult or sensitive topics. As a result, models may perform less well on easier classes, neglecting important but less contentious tasks.

### **Counterfactual Regret Minimization**
#### Definition
A loss function designed to minimize regret by comparing observed outcomes to their counterfactual alternatives.
#### Explanation
In essence, this concept measures the difference between actual and hypothetical outcomes under different scenarios. By using this approach in training models, we can identify which actions led to suboptimal results and make informed decisions for future iterations.

### **Unsupervised Knowledge Distillation**
#### Definition
A method of knowledge transfer that enables models to acquire generalizable features without human guidance.
#### Explanation
This process involves training a smaller model on the output of a pre-trained, more complex model. By aligning their weights and behaviors through minimization, the smaller models learn a distilled version of the larger model's capabilities, allowing them to generalize across a wide range of tasks.
