## Abbreviations

### New additions:

#### Terms with 0 previous additions:
* **BROCKMAN**: A process or system for evaluating the suitability of a candidate or project.
	+ The Brockman method uses a combination of assessment tools and expert evaluation to determine a candidate's fit for a role, ensuring that only qualified candidates move forward in the hiring process. This systematic approach helps organizations streamline their recruitment efforts, reducing bias and improving the overall quality of hire.
* **CAVERN**: A type of digital repository designed to store and manage large amounts of structured data.
	+ The cavern is built with advanced indexing features and utilizes machine learning algorithms to optimize its search functionality, making it easily navigable by domain experts. This enables researchers to quickly locate critical data within the cavern, streamlining their research workflow.

#### Terms with previous additions:
* **CVV**: Card Verification Value
	+ Verified to ensure a secure credit card transaction. Used for verifying the identity of card holders and ensuring all transactions go through properly.
 
#### Definitions for existing terms:
* **PRNG** (Algorithmic Random Number Generator)
	+ Used in cryptography for generating strong random numbers, PRNG helps ensure security by producing unpredictable outputs that would not be susceptible to predicting patterns or brute-force attacks. This process is used to create unique keys and verify user identities.
