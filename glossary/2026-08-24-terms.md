# Weekly AI/ML Glossary Terms (2026-08-24)

Added 3 AI/ML terms to /tmp/glossary.md:

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Definition: Retrieval-augmented generation is an AI technique that retrieves relevant external information and supplies it to a language model before generating a response.

Explanation: RAG can ground answers in current, private, or domain-specific documents rather than relying only on model parameters. Its quality depends on both retrieving the right evidence and accurately using that evidence in the generated answer.

## Mixture of Experts (MoE)
Definition: A mixture-of-experts model is a neural network that routes each input through a selected subset of specialized expert modules.

Explanation: Sparse routing lets an MoE model increase its total parameter count without activating every parameter for every token. This can improve computational efficiency at inference time, although routing, load balancing, and serving complexity become important engineering concerns.

## Diffusion Model
Definition: A diffusion model is a generative model that learns to create data by progressively denoising a corrupted sample.

Explanation: During training, the model learns how noise is added and then predicts the steps needed to reverse that process. Diffusion models are widely used for image, audio, and video generation because they can produce detailed outputs from learned distributions.
