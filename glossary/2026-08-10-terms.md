# Weekly AI/ML Glossary Terms (2026-08-10)

## Test-Time Compute

Definition: Test-time compute is the computational effort a model uses to refine an answer after receiving a prompt.

Explanation: Increasing this effort can let a model explore alternatives, verify intermediate steps, or correct mistakes before responding. It can improve performance on complex tasks without changing the model’s trained parameters.

## Mechanistic Interpretability

Definition: Mechanistic interpretability is the study of how a neural network’s internal components produce its behavior.

Explanation: Researchers analyze features, circuits, attention patterns, and activations to identify how information is represented and transformed. The goal is to explain model behavior at a deeper level than observing inputs and outputs alone.

## Representation Engineering

Definition: Representation engineering is the practice of identifying and modifying a model’s internal representations to influence its behavior.

Explanation: It may involve detecting activation patterns associated with concepts such as truthfulness, emotion, or refusal. Those patterns can then be strengthened, weakened, or redirected during inference without fully retraining the model.
