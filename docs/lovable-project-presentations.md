# Math Mania and Gym Scholar feature handoff

Each standalone page has a `data-feature-card` article containing its name,
one-line description, real screenshot, Live badge, and direct launch action.
The shared presentation uses `forest-design.css` and the Forest shell's
typography, panel, navigation, focus, and status components, with a small shared
layout in `web/shared/lovable-project.css`.

| Entry | Page | One line | Screenshot | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Math Mania | `/web/math-mania/` | Playful multiplication practice with trail challenges, visual tutorials, and guessing puzzles. | `/web/assets/lovable-projects/math-mania.png` | Live |
| Gym Scholar | `/web/gym-scholar/` | Evidence-based strength training with interactive muscle targeting and research-linked training rules. | `/web/assets/lovable-projects/gym-scholar.png` | Live |

Math Mania retains its playable Lovable embed and a direct full-screen fallback
at https://forest-math-plus.lovable.app. Gym Scholar launches
https://gymscholar.lovable.app directly. Both apps and sampled public interactions
were verified on 12 October 2026; screenshot provenance is in
`web/assets/lovable-projects/README.md`.

This handoff does not select homepage placement, change the front-page index or
pool designs, split the settled Math Mania / Forest Math catalog identity, or
claim catalog readiness review approval. The existing catalog's provisional and
review fields stay as recorded. The redesign can reuse these prepared articles
and assets when it chooses featured placement.
