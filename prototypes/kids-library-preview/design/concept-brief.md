# Second visual exploration

Self-authored art direction under the user's request for three genuinely unique suggestions. Product facts are confirmed in the conversation; visual decisions below are proposals, not approvals.

## Scope

Three independent image previews. Each is one plausible static HTML/CSS/JS movie library page with Movies and Books as separate sibling destinations. No catalogue of books is fabricated. Nothing is implemented or deployed at this stage.

## Subject inventory

SD Forest name; parent choosing alongside a child; separate media; research chapters; global countries; film runtimes; age bands; themes such as courage and kindness; candid content notes; provenance; rewatching; future author/illustrator and reading-length metadata.

## Eight topics

1. Vibe: three proposals, rather than one requested house style. Daylight botanical fieldwork; graphic cultural magazine; dimensional paper theatre.
2. Journey: orient in Movies or Books, choose age/theme, compare actual works, read the honest note, check the viewing country.
3. Energy: a distinct opening followed by calm readable entries.
4. Feeling curve: curiosity from the visual identity, agency from immediate browsing, confidence from full parent guidance, readiness from an availability action.
5. Signature seed: keep the same age and theme context when switching between separate movie and book libraries.
6. Range: editorial, graphic, illustrated. Each uses different type, palette, image treatment, and geometry.
7. Structure: distinct sections and cuts; no continuous camera journey or forced scrolling.
8. Assets: research content and existing code/tokens were inspected earlier. New image previews use original generated illustration; shipping assets will require a separate production pass.

## Peak

The visitor recognizes a story's emotional value and hard moments together, before committing to it. This is the detailed first work, not a decorative hero.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where I can find a story for my child's age and feelings, then switch from watching to reading without starting the search over.

## Truth and visualization

The analytical job is categorical discovery and comparison. Age is an editorial recommendation with a reason; themes are categories, not measured scores. Use direct labels and accessible controls. No fake ratings, intensity gauges, invented distribution counts, geographic availability maps, or charts without evidence. Manual country selection, no location permission.

## Mobile and motion

Each proposal must have ordinary document flow and a thumb-friendly small-screen path. Search and filters collapse while the first work remains nearby. Any scroll motion is optional, short, and uses a complete reduced-motion state. No hero gate before reaching the catalogue.
