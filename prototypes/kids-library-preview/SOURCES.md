# Sources for the Kids’ Library preview

The active source-of-truth is the per-title [Iris verification ledger](src/researchVerification.js), which records status, corrected metadata, and the source URLs returned by eight search batches. The Cool Kids Animation Filmography is retained in `src/researchCatalog.js` only as a candidate seed and is not used as verified content.

- **My Neighbor Totoro (1988)** — “Studio Ghibli: Meditative Pacing, Animism, and Unhurried Wonder.” [Studio Ghibli](https://www.ghibli.jp/works/totoro/) identifies Hayao Miyazaki, the 1988 release, and an approximately 86-minute runtime. The [BBFC](https://www.bbfc.co.uk/release/my-neighbor-totoro-q29sbgvjdglvbjpwwc0yotgzndc) describes the worries about the girls’ ill mother and a child briefly going missing.
- **The Iron Giant (1999)** — “The Classical and Contemporary American Canon: Walt Disney and Pixar.” [Warner Bros.](https://www.warnerbros.it/scheda-film/genere-animazione/il-gigante-di-ferro/) lists Brad Bird and an 86-minute runtime; the research supplies the 1999 year. The [BBFC](https://www.bbfc.co.uk/release/the-iron-giant-q29sbgvjdglvbjpwwc00mdg3mjm) describes the action violence and threatening scenes.
- **Ernest & Celestine (2012)** — “The European, Franco-Belgian, and Irish Auteur Renaissance.” [Film Fund Luxembourg](https://filmfund.lu/en/catalogue/film/ernest-and-celestine/) lists the directors, 80-minute runtime, and the credited producers. The [BBFC](https://www.bbfc.co.uk/release/ernest-et-celestine-ernest-and-celestine-q29sbgvjdglvbjpwwc00mzq0odu) describes brief threats and frightening moments.

Age bands, reasons, themes, and content notes are editorial guidance, not verbatim research or official age ratings. The research’s suggested audience profiles differ from the preview’s bands in places; parents should use the notes and their knowledge of their child when choosing.

The 63-card catalog uses the Iris ledger for title, year, director, and studio/producer fields. The current pass independently resolved 143 unique source URLs with no 404/410 result; two Cartoon Saloon URLs required GET rather than HEAD and returned HTTP 200 after redirect. Thirteen records are flagged, twelve corrected, and thirty-eight verified. Premise, visual form, audience profile, age band, content note, and unsupported studio/director details remain explicitly unverified rather than being copied from the model-written seed. Filter themes are a lightweight editorial index and are not ratings.

The three local poster samples are Wikimedia-hosted files surfaced from the corresponding English Wikipedia file pages: [My Neighbor Totoro poster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:My_Neighbor_Totoro_-_Tonari_no_Totoro_(Movie_Poster).jpg), [The Iron Giant poster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Iron_Giant_poster.JPG), and [Ernest & Celestine poster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Ernest_%26_Celestine_poster.jpg). They are used only as the local default-poster side of the flip; they are not generated catalog artwork. Review licensing before any public launch, and do not bulk-add official posters without a rights decision.

The “Check availability” control opens a country-specific JustWatch search for the film. It is a lookup, not a promise that a service carries the title or that it is available to a particular viewer. The preview offers the 18 countries shown in its selector.
