# Senior design review: pool entries (`feat/pool-entries`, draft PR #694), 2026-10-04

Reviewed against Ivan's 2026-10-01 direction:

- The pools and their entries are the redesign target. Entries should sit inside higher-quality imagery, and any tree-trunk framing should be subtle.

## Critique, per pool

All six are clearly authored. Each has its own palette, type, layout and motion language, and they sit on one shared entry runtime. This is well above the "flat clip-art" bar. The notes below are about the gap between good and premium.

**Artificial Self (settled concept): the strongest of the six.**
- What works: a violet-black specimen plate, with the teal neuron and the copper die meeting at a glowing lilac seam. Grain, bokeh and the frame ticks give it depth. The phone composition (neuron stacked over circuit) is its own composition, not a squeezed desktop.
- Reads basic: the dendrites are uniform hairlines with no taper or branching hierarchy, which looks more like noise than tissue up close.
- Imagery quality: high.

**My Story (settled concept): honest and charming.**
- What works: graphite on warm paper, the wrong last stroke, the red scribble fix, and real-looking pencils and eraser.
- Reads basic: the paper itself is flat, and the red "shorter." note is very faint at most of the loop's phases. At the end of the pin the stage dissolves to blank paper for roughly 100 px of scroll before the notebook arrives. It is short, but it is a quiet beat, not motion.
- Imagery quality: good, intentionally minimal.

**GrowingApp (proposed concept): warm and sunlit.**
- What works: window light, a nicely drawn seedling, and the doorframe height marks. The page body keeps the doorframe casing as a left-edge rule, which is already the structural equivalent of "a trunk at the side", and it works because it is quiet.
- Reads basic: the door and floorboards are flat vector fills next to the softer wall and plant.

**AI-d kit (proposed concept): convincing hardware.**
- What works: a brushed-graphite rack, a patch bay with a real cable seat, and honest "?" price slots.
- Reads basic: the jacks are identical clones, with no per-jack wear or light falloff, so the grid reads as a pattern.

**TinkerBox (proposed concept): a premium-leaning bench.**
- What works: an axonometric gear train on a glass plate, a chalk grid and one spare screw.
- Reads basic: the orange notice tag is the loudest element on the stage and competes with the headline.

**Design Gallery (proposed concept): hushed and well lit.**
- What works: three arched doorways with floor reflections; one-point perspective done right.
- Reads basic: the wall artworks are grey placeholder rectangles in heavy black frames, the weakest imagery on any of the six pages. There was also a real bug, now fixed: the back link's hairline struck through the capitals.

**Consistency.** One entry system and one timing discipline are applied everywhere. Health runs on a separate three.js runtime (its own branch), so the seven pools do not yet share one entry system. That is flagged as a decision below.

**Trunk framing.** None of the six entries has a trunk today, so there is nothing to keep subtle. GrowingApp's casing shows how a subtle side frame could work.

## Proposal for Ivan (not built; needs your go)

1. **Subtle trunk framing, one per pool, in that pool's own material.**
   - One trunk at one side edge, at 5 to 8% contrast against the ground, never behind copy, and hidden below about 900 px wide.
   - Each pool draws it in its own language: a graphite line trunk in My Story, a birch-white casing-trunk in GrowingApp (replacing the current casing rule), a brushed-metal rack post that grows bark texture in AI-d kit, a chalk-outline trunk in TinkerBox, a plaster column with a faint grain in Design Gallery, and a dendrite-like trunk in Artificial Self.
   - It ties every pool back to the forest without a shared "forest skin". I would prototype it on one pool first, GrowingApp, since the casing is already there.
2. **Catalog sections that sit inside imagery.**
   - Today every pool's "Everything in …" list sits on a plain ground under the entry. The next step is to carry each entry's imagery down behind the list at low intensity: the gallery floor reflection under Design Gallery's list, the rack continuing behind AI-d kit's table, paper grain and margin rule under My Story.
   - This is the pool-imagery overhaul Ivan asked to pace, so it is written up here and not built.
3. **Imagery upgrades where the render is weakest.**
   - Design Gallery's wall artworks: code-drawn abstract works with real texture, not grey rectangles.
   - AI-d kit's jack grid: per-jack light falloff and wear.
   - Artificial Self's dendrites: tapered and hierarchically branched.
   - Each is small on its own, but together they change the look, so they need your go.
4. **One entry runtime for all seven pools.** Either Health moves onto `pool-entry.mjs` (with a WebGL scene adapter), or it stays separate by design.
