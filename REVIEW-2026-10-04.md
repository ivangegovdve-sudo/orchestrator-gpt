# Senior design review — Health pool (`feat/sdm-61-health-world`, PR #679), 2026-10-04

Reviewed against Ivan's 2026-10-01 direction: the front page is untouched (not opened in this run), the pools and their entries are the redesign target, imagery should be higher quality and any trunk framing kept subtle, and the pace is slow: refine, don't overhaul.

## Critique

**What reads premium**
- The hero's structure is right. One large code-rendered object beside a confident display headline, a dark forest-green ground, and a single scroll gesture (the dolly into the cutaway). Nothing is labelled and nothing is faked.
- The motion holds up. Breathing, drift, Golgi budding and ribosome streaming are all explicit functions of time and scroll, and they are slow enough to read as alive rather than busy. Under reduced motion the page is genuinely still.
- The close act ("Enter a project. Tell it what is missing.") is quiet and well spaced.

**What read basic (before this pass)**
- **Palette, which was most of the "glossy and basic" verdict.** A saturated toy-cyan membrane, a hot-pink nucleus, lime-green mitochondria and a flat beige floor: the school-textbook colour key. It fought the forest ground instead of belonging to it. Green mitochondria also read as chloroplasts, which is an accuracy problem.
- **Plastic gloss and flat lighting.** Clearcoat sat on almost every material, and the cut faces had a strong white emissive, so the cytoplasm and chromatin lost their shading and looked like printed stickers.
- **The seam.** The pinned dark cell ended in a hard horizontal cut onto the bone statement section.
- **The catalog rail is now the weakest part of the page.** Flat paper cards with a ring doodle are clean but generic, and a step below the cell. Left as is; see the proposal.
- **The close-up still shows its construction.** Up close the rough ER reads as crinkled ribbon and the ribosomes as scattered beads. This improved but is not solved, and solving it is a modelling job rather than a polish job.

**Entry inside high-quality imagery.** The cell is the only real imagery on the page, and the entry copy sits well beside it. The specimens rail drops out of that world into a plain bone panel, so the page reads as "great hero, then a catalog". There is no trunk framing on this page, so there is nothing to keep subtle.

## What I changed (one commit, targeted)

1. **Reviewer finding 1 (noscript).** A `<noscript><style>` now makes every `[data-sc-cue]` visible without the engine, hides the "Loading catalog…" placeholder and shows the notice. The notice now says honestly what does not run without JavaScript. Its required prefix is kept for the structure test.
2. **Reviewer finding 2 (keyboard focus in the close act).** A page-level `focusin` handler in `pool.js` runs after the engine's own handler. When a focused cue inside a pinned act is still dark, it scrolls that act to the middle of the cue's lit window. The engine stays byte-identical to `.agents/skills/scroll-craft/engine/`. Verified: tabbing to "Return to the specimens" from the top of the page lands with the link in view at opacity 1.
3. **Cell material and palette pass (`cell.js`).**
   - Membrane: desaturated teal-glass instead of toy cyan.
   - Nucleus: deep plum, with finer chromatin.
   - Mitochondria: coral, the conventional colour and no chloroplast confusion.
   - Golgi, ER, vesicles and ribosomes are quieter. Ribosomes are smaller.
   - Clearcoat and cut-face emissive are lower, so the faces keep their light.
   - Geometry, motion and camera are unchanged.
4. **Seam.** A 24vh bone gradient on `.clinic-statement::before` dissolves the cell into the statement instead of a hard cut.
5. `scrollcraft/builds/health/` copies re-synced (they were byte-identical before), and the review sheets were replaced with this run's shots.

## Proposal for Ivan (not built — needs your go)

1. **Put the specimens inside the cell's world.** Instead of the bone paper rail, the catalog would sit on a dark, softly lit continuation of the cell interior (cytoplasm-toned ground, depth haze, a few slow drifting organelles out of focus behind the cards). The cards would become frosted "slides" with the project's colour as a thin stain line. This is the "entries sitting inside higher-quality imagery" move for Health. It changes the page's light/dark rhythm, so it is your call.
2. **Subtle trunk framing.** One very low-contrast trunk silhouette at the left edge of the hero and the close act, about 6–8% luminance over the ground and never in front of copy. It would tie Health to the forest without competing with the cell. I would prototype it as a CSS/SVG layer behind the canvas, shown only ≥ 1100 px wide.
3. **A modelling pass on the close-up**, rather than more polish: smooth ER as continuous tubules, ribosomes as fewer, smaller clusters on the rough ER, and a true depth-of-field pass for the zoomed frames. Free and code-only, but a day of work and worth doing only if 1 and 2 are a yes.
