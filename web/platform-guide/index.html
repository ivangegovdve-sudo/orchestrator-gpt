Harnesses

Expired
Vercel AI SDK
The standard if you're building in React/Next.js.
Good At
Abstracts streaming perfectly. RSC integration is magic. Easy switching between providers.
Bad At
Heavily ties you to Vercel's ecosystem and Next.js paradigms. Doing things outside their happy path (like custom memory management) is painful.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source).
Verified: 2024-05-20 Expires: 2023-12-01
Expired
LangChain (JS/Python)
The monolith everyone uses then complains about.
Good At
Incredible amount of integrations. If a tool exists, LangChain has a loader for it.
Bad At
Overly complex abstractions. You spend more time fighting LangChain classes than writing AI logic. Bloated.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source). LangSmith is paid.
Verified: 2024-04-10 Expires: 2023-12-01
Expired

Better than LangChain if your primary focus is RAG.
Good At
Excellent data ingestion and indexing strategies. Actually understands how chunking should work.
Bad At
Still suffers from heavy abstraction. Hard to debug when retrieval fails.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source).
Verified: 2024-05-05 Expires: 2023-12-01

Memory solutions and upgrades

Stale
Mem0
A decent drop-in memory layer if you don't want to build it yourself.
Good At
Handles entity extraction and temporal memory out of the box. Simple API.
Bad At
Black box retrieval. You can't tune the embedding weights easily if it returns the wrong context.
Pricing & Hosting
Free tier available. Usage based after.
Verified: 2024-05-10
Stale
Zep
Built specifically for chat history and agent memory.
Good At
Fast. Handles summarization of older context windows automatically.
Bad At
Self-hosting can be heavy. Their proprietary retrieval algorithms aren't always transparent.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source) or cloud pricing starting around $20/mo.
Verified: 2024-04-15
Stale
Pinecone
The default vector database, but overkill for many.
Good At
Fully managed, scales instantly. Serverless offering is great for intermittent loads.
Bad At
Not a 'memory' solution, just a database. You have to write all the chunking, embedding, and retrieval logic.
Pricing & Hosting
Serverless is pay-per-read/write. Pods start at $70/mo.
Verified: 2024-05-01
Stale
Weaviate
My preferred vector DB if I need hybrid search.
Good At
BM25 + Vector search works beautifully out of the box. Good self-hosting.
Bad At
Cloud pricing can jump quickly. The API can feel over-engineered for simple tasks.
Pricing & Hosting
Open source free. Cloud starts around $25/mo.
Verified: 2024-03-20
Stale
Chroma
Best for local development and small apps.
Good At
Runs locally with zero setup. Integrates with everything.
Bad At
Distributed/cloud offering has been 'coming soon' forever. Not for large scale production.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source).
Verified: 2024-05-18
Stale

Very fast. Good built-in summarization.
Bad At
Development seems to have slowed down. Small community.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source).
Verified: 2023-10-15
Stale
Qdrant
The rising star of vector databases. Rust-based and fast.
Good At
Incredible performance. Great filtering capabilities alongside vector search.
Bad At
Complex configuration options can be daunting for beginners.
Pricing & Hosting
Open source free. Cloud free tier is generous.
Verified: 2024-05-12
Stale
Postgres (pgvector)
What you should probably actually use if you already have a database.
Good At
You don't need a new database. Joins relational data with vectors effortlessly.
Bad At
Performance at extreme scale (100M+ vectors) requires tuning. Not a 'memory' API, just raw storage.
Pricing & Hosting
Free (Open source).
Verified: 2024-05-22
