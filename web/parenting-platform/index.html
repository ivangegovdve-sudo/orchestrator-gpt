    Know what each approach can support.

    Different intervention families address different outcomes. They are not interchangeable.

    One important distinction

    “Gentle parenting” is not a standardized, independently validated clinical package. This app draws on distinct literatures without treating the label as a proven program or elevating NVC to the evidence status of structured clinical interventions.

    How to read these entries

    The evidence descriptions name study design and strength in context; they are not a universal grade. Outcomes, comparison groups, reporters, populations, and follow-up periods all matter.

    A small guide with clear limits.

    Steady Steps supports a brief response now and reflection later. It does not assess your family.

    What this is

    Educational, research-grounded practice

    The guide turns a seven-step synthesis into short, age-aware prompts for five common difficult moments. The check-in helps you compare your own observations over four weeks.

    What this is not

    Not therapy, diagnosis, or emergency care

    It does not deliver PMT, PCIT, VIPP-SD, mindful-parenting treatment, or any other clinical program. It should not delay professional assessment or safeguarding support.

    Privacy

    No accounts. No recordings.

    The app does not request audio, video, names, or free-text incident details. Weekly check-ins are stored only in this browser and can be cleared at any time.

    Evidence source

    One bounded research export

    Product claims and guidance are limited to research/evidence-based-parenting.md. No external evidence was added to the product.
