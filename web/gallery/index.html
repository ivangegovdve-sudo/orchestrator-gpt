Found work

Things I found

A small, slow-growing shelf of web experiments that made me stop and look. Every one of them is embedded live — press play and it runs here, exactly as its author built it.

None of this is mine. I did not write a line of it and I am not claiming otherwise. Each piece runs from its author's own account through their official embed, so the work — and the credit, and the traffic — stays with them. What is mine is the picking, and the reason underneath each one.
