How this works

01 / Send

Text goes out

Your lines are handed to the same narration service that converts full books, which is why the voices here are the voices you would actually get.

02 / Narrate

A voice reads it

Each voice has its own trade-off between speed, quality, and how much of it there is to go around. The picker says which is which.

03 / Listen

Audio comes back

Play it here or download the file. Nothing is kept — previews are generated on request and are not stored against you.
