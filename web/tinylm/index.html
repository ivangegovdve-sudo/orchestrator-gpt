Why "consciousness experiment"? The observer seat's only job is to watch the other models think and describe what it sees — a tiny machine reflecting on machine reasoning as it happens. The models are charmingly scrappy: expect flashes of insight wrapped in small-model quirks, not polished GPT answers. That contrast is the experiment.
