🌱 TinyLM Consciousness Experiment

Five tiny open language models — 135M to 3B parameters, small enough to run on a single ARM server — deliberate on your question in sequence, each seat playing a different cognitive role. Every token is generated on our own silicon and streamed here live. No cloud LLM APIs, no fleet memory, no hidden context: what you see is the entire conversation.

🧠 The five seats
Fast Proposer
Tiny-Agent 0.5B
Fires off the first draft answer — quick, unfiltered, instinctive.
Analyst
llama3.2:1b
Unpacks the question and the draft — what is actually being asked?
Critic
qwen2.5:0.5b

Consciousness Observer
eve
Watches the deliberation itself and comments on how the council is thinking.
Synthesizer
qwen2.5:3b
Weaves every seat's contribution into the council's final answer.

Why “consciousness experiment”? The observer seat's only job is to watch the other models think and describe what it sees — a tiny machine reflecting on machine reasoning as it happens. The models are charmingly scrappy: expect flashes of insight wrapped in small-model quirks, not polished GPT answers. That contrast is the experiment.
