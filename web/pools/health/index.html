About this pool

Health tools that fit the person using them, and stay free.

What this pool is for

Reading should not depend on how your eyes and attention happen to work.

Health starts with access: your own books, set in the typeface, spacing, colour and pace that suit you, or read aloud when reading is hard. Every reading control is free, and the standard narration voices have no usage limit. Each tool keeps its evidence with it, rather than in a central claims page.

Movement sessions shaped around your day, and a research base on women's health, are next.

Reading and listening tools

Two separate implementations: reading accessibility and audiobook production. Select a tab when scripting is available; both sections remain readable without it.

Read your own ebooks your way.

Drop in an EPUB, PDF or text file and chapters are found for you. Typeface, spacing, colour, line focus and narration are all adjustable, and every one of those controls stays free.

Dyslexia Reading Platform

A separate reading environment with accessibility controls. Its existing implementation is identified by the shared catalog.

Turn a book into an audiobook.

Upload an EPUB, PDF or text file up to 50 MB and get back narration with every chapter bookmarked, so you can pick up where you left off. The standard voices are free and unlimited, which makes full-length books practical.

Audiobook Studio

A separate audiobook production tool. Its existing implementation is identified by the shared catalog.
