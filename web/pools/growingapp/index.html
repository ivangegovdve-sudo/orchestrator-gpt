GrowingApp

About this pool

Raising children with the research at hand. This pool is where what studies say about how children grow becomes something a family can use.

What this pool is for

Parents get advice from every direction, and most of it arrives without a source.

GrowingApp starts from the other end: the published research on child development and parenting, read and organised so that a piece of guidance can point to the studies behind it, and say plainly where the evidence stops. The app is how that reaches a family.

The evidence base is built next, from peer-reviewed studies.

Learning side by side

The first things here are for a parent and child to do together.

Cubeflow

Show it your cube one face at a time. It checks the scan makes a real cube, finds a solution, and teaches every turn.

Interactive Periodic Table

Bulgarian and English, pitched at about age eleven. Tap an element for its compounds, or highlight everything inside a battery, a banana or a smartphone.

Math Mania

A trail of stages that starts easy and grows with the player, free practice with no score pressure, and puzzles where guessing wrong is the fun part.
