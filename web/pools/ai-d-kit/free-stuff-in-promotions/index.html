Standing free tiers

These do not expire, so instead each one shows the date it was last confirmed. Past 90 days without a recheck a tier keeps its place and gains a visible STALE marker — it is usually still real, but the date is the thing you should be reading, because a shrinking free tier can end up costing more than an ended promotion.
