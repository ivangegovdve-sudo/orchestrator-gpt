TinkerBox

About this pool

A public workbench. Things get built here to find out how they work, and stay open while that is being figured out.

What this pool is for

The quickest way to understand a thing is to take it apart where others can watch.

TinkerBox holds the working parts behind the rest of the forest: the engine that draws Chloé's avatar, and a panel of models you can put a question to. Each one is live and usable, and each shows its workings rather than hiding them.

A printable calendar maker and a game-icon generator are next on the bench.

Avatar Playground

Chloé is a cloud of dots joined by a shifting web, not a face and not an orb. This is the real engine she runs on, with every state, expression and rule one click away.
