About this pool

Things to poke at: the engine behind Chloé's avatar, a panel of models you can question, and a few tools still on the bench.

Avatar Playground

Chloé is a cloud of dots joined by a shifting web, not a face and not an orb. This is the real engine she runs on, with every state, expression and rule one click away: idle, listening, thinking, speaking.

Nothing runs until you press start. It drives the cloud from real sound and her own voice.

Model councils

Ask one hard question under two very different conditions. Both are public and stateless: no fleet memory, no delegation, nothing saved. The same council is shared with AI-d kit.

TinyLLM Local Oracle
Five small local models on Oracle ARM hardware, each with a fixed role: proposer, analyst, critic, observer and synthesizer. Slow by design, and free.
OpenRouter Free
Free cloud models take distinct deliberation roles. The roster is rechecked daily, and a model keeps its seat only if it actually answered a real call in the past week.

In development

Working tools that are not signed off yet.

Calendar Generator
A printable cover and thirteen month pages with your own images and public holidays for your country, in A4 or A5.
Item Icon Generator
Turns a seed image and a short description into a stylised game-item icon.
