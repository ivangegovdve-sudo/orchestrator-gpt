About

Forest HUB is a family workshop on the open web — a single place where our tools, games, and experiments live. Reference apps like the AI Glossary and Platforms Map sit next to kids' games, printable calendars, poetry, and scroll-driven stories. Everything here is hand-built: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the front, a small FastAPI backend behind it.

New projects land as self-contained pages under /web/, so each card above is a complete little world of its own. Source lives on GitHub.
