SDForest is Ivan Gegov’s living collection of writing, creative projects, practical web tools, and research experiments. It is for curious readers, makers, learners, and families—choose one of seven live pools below.

SDForest is an evolving collection. Experimental and in-development work is labeled plainly; every live path should lead somewhere real.

  • Open Dashboard MCP↗ (npm package, external site)

  • Troubleshooting record

  • Repo Shelf↗ (external site)

    1,192 public repositories as books on a 3D shelf. Search them, browse by shelf, open any one.

  • The Drop↗ (external site)

    A daily AI publication. Today’s pick first, then what still matters from earlier drops.
